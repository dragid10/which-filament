## When to pick it

You want PETG's weather and heat resistance with extra stiffness: drone and RC frames, enclosure brackets, printer upgrade parts, and structural parts that must hold shape. Carbon fiber also reduces stringing, so prints often look cleaner than plain PETG.

## Watch out for

- It wears brass nozzles quickly. Use hardened steel.
- Less impact-tolerant than plain PETG. It trades some toughness for stiffness.
- Costs noticeably more.

## Tips

Dry it thoroughly and start from the manufacturer's profile. Heat resistance is better than plain PETG, but check the datasheet if it needs to survive very hot places.
