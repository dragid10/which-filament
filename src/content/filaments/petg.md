## Use it for

- Parts that need to work, not just look good: garage organizers, car interior parts, planters, bike mounts, water bottle holders, and snap-fit housings.

## Watch out for

- Stringing and "zits" unless retraction and temperature are tuned.
- It bonds *very* well to smooth PEI plates. Use a thin layer of glue stick or a release agent so it does not pull chunks out of the surface.
- Absorbs moisture quickly, which shows up as bubbling and rough surfaces.
- Not for years in full sun: UV can yellow and weaken it, especially in light or clear colors. Use [ASA](/filaments/asa) for that.

## Tips

- Lower the part cooling compared to PLA for stronger layers.
- Keep it sealed with desiccant between prints.
- Printing a lot? Try [Rapid PETG](/filaments/rapid-petg).
