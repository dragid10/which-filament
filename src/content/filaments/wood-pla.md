## Use it for

- Signs, picture frames, decorative boxes, and gifts where a wood look matters.
- You can sand and stain it like real wood.

## Watch out for

- Wood fibers clog small nozzles. Use 0.5 mm or larger.
- Too hot and the fibers scorch, giving a burnt smell and dark streaks. Stay on the low side of the range.
- It is weak and brittle. Do not use it for anything that takes load.

## Tips

- Vary temperature across a test tower for lighter or darker tones.
- Sand lightly and seal with wax or oil for a wood finish.
