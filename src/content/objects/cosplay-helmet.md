---
title: Cosplay helmet or armor
summary: Big, visible, painted, and worn for a day.
useCases: [cosplay, large]
best: matte-pla
runnersUp: [pla-plus, rapid-pla]
avoid: [silk-pla]
---

Large pieces are easier in PLA because it warps less, and matte PLA gives a surface that sands and paints cleanly. Print in sections and glue them.

Use PLA+ for pieces that get handled and strapped on. Silk is too brittle for something worn and bumped, and its shine fights with paint. If you will wear it in a hot car or outside in summer, consider PETG.
