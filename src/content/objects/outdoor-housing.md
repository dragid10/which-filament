---
title: Outdoor sensor or camera housing
summary: Years of sun, rain, and temperature swings.
useCases: [outdoor, waterproof, hot-environment]
best: asa
runnersUp: [asa-cf, petg]
avoid: [abs, pla, silk-pla, matte-pla]
---

ASA is built for this. It does not yellow or crack in sunlight, and it handles the heat of a sun-baked enclosure.

PETG is a decent budget option for a shaded spot. ABS looks similar but breaks down in sun over time.
