---
title: Gears, hinges, and snap-fits
summary: Moving parts that flex, rub, and carry load.
useCases: [mechanical, functional]
best: petg
runnersUp: [pla-plus]
avoid: [silk-pla, pla, rapid-pla]
---

Parts that flex or take repeated load need toughness. PETG bends before it breaks and has strong layer adhesion, so snap-fits and living hinges last. PLA+ is a good alternative when you want a stiffer, easier print.

Plain and silk PLA are brittle: thin snap-fits tend to crack the first few times they flex.
