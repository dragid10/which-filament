---
title: Keychain or holiday ornament
summary: Small, decorative, and handled a lot.
useCases: [display]
best: sparkle-pla
runnersUp: [silk-pla, matte-pla, pla-plus]
avoid: [wood-pla]
---

- Sparkle PLA adds shine with almost no printing difficulty.
- Make the hanging loop thick so it does not snap.
- Use PLA+ if the piece will be handled hard, such as a keychain that lives in a pocket.
