---
title: Lamp shade or light diffuser
summary: Thin walls that glow when an LED is behind them.
useCases: [display]
best: pla
runnersUp: [clear-petg, matte-pla]
avoid: [wood-pla, pla-cf]
---

- Natural or white PLA with thin walls scatters light softly and is easy to print.
- Print it a little thin for a diffused glow.
- Clear PETG is a good choice if the LED runs warm or you want a glassier look.
- Opaque or fiber-filled filaments, such as carbon fiber or wood, block the light.
