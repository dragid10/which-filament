---
title: Shower or bathroom hook
summary: Warm, wet, and humid every day.
useCases: [waterproof, hot-environment]
best: petg
runnersUp: [rapid-petg, clear-petg]
avoid: [pla, silk-pla, matte-pla]
---

PETG tolerates warm, humid air and repeated wetting much better than PLA, which can soften with heat and slowly degrade with moisture. Print thick and orient layers so the load does not pull them apart.
