---
title: Stone-look bookend or desk statue
summary: A heavy-looking decorative piece that should look like stone.
useCases: [display]
best: marble-pla
runnersUp: [matte-pla, pla]
avoid: []
---

- Marble or stone-effect PLA gives a natural speckled finish with no painting.
- The pattern hides layer lines.
- Add weight (sand or coins in a sealed cavity) so it feels like stone.
- Matte PLA is the plain alternative if you will paint it.
