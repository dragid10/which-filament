---
title: Wood-look sign or picture frame
summary: A decorative piece meant to look like wood.
useCases: [display, cosplay]
best: wood-pla
runnersUp: [matte-pla]
avoid: []
---

- Wood PLA gives a warm grain-like look and can be sanded and stained.
- Use a 0.5 mm or larger nozzle and keep the temperature low to avoid scorching.
- If you prefer easier printing, a brown matte PLA gives a similar look with no clog risk.
