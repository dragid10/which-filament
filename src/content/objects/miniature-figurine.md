---
title: Miniature or figurine
summary: Fine detail, painted or displayed on a shelf.
useCases: [display]
best: pla
runnersUp: [matte-pla, rapid-pla]
avoid: [silk-pla, petg]
---

- Plain PLA gives the crispest fine detail and is the easiest to paint.
- Matte PLA is a good choice if you will not paint it.
- Silk and PETG both soften small features: silk's gloss hides detail, and PETG tends to string between small parts.
