---
title: Gasket, seal, or vibration foot
summary: Squishes to seal or damp vibration.
useCases: [flexible, functional]
best: tpu-85a
runnersUp: [tpu-95a]
avoid: [pla, petg, abs]
---

A soft TPU conforms to uneven surfaces and compresses to seal. 85A is more squishy, while 95A is easier to print and holds more load.

Check the temperature the seal will see. TPU handles moderate heat, but it will not suit a hot engine bay.
