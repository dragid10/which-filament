---
title: RC tires or grips
summary: Rubbery parts that take abrasion and stay grippy.
useCases: [flexible, mechanical]
best: tpu-95a
runnersUp: [tpu-85a]
avoid: [pla, petg]
---

TPU wears slowly and keeps its grip. 95A is firm enough to hold a tire shape on a hub. 85A grips better but may deform at high speed.
