---
title: 3D printer upgrade parts
summary: Fan ducts, brackets, and spool holders that sit near a hot printer.
useCases: [hot-environment, functional]
best: petg
runnersUp: [petg-cf, rapid-petg]
avoid: [pla, pla-plus, silk-pla]
---

Parts near the hotend or heated bed exceed PLA's softening point, so use PETG or better. A fan duct that droops over time is a common failure with PLA.

For parts right beside the hotend, check whether you need something even more heat-resistant than PETG.
