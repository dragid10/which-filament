---
title: Garage hooks and shelf brackets
summary: Hold tools or boxes day in, day out, in a space that gets hot and cold.
useCases: [functional, mechanical, hot-environment]
best: petg
runnersUp: [pla-plus]
avoid: [silk-pla, matte-pla]
---

Constant load plus a garage that swings between freezing and hot calls for PETG: it resists creep and softening better than PLA. Print with extra walls and orient layers so the load does not pull them apart.

For a heated, indoor space with light loads, PLA+ is cheaper and easier.
