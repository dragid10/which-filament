---
title: Car phone mount or vent clip
summary: A dashboard in direct sun averages about 70 °C; cabin air about 45 °C.
useCases: [hot-environment, functional]
best: petg
runnersUp: [rapid-petg, asa, abs]
avoid: [pla, pla-plus, matte-pla, silk-pla, rapid-pla, rapid-pla-plus]
sources:
  - title: "ASU study: hot cars can hit dangerous temperatures within an hour"
    url: "https://asunow.asu.edu/20180516-discoveries-asu-study-hot-cars-can-hit-deadly-temperatures-within-one-hour"
---

- This is the classic case where PLA fails: a dashboard in summer sun passes PLA's softening point and the mount droops or warps.
- PETG handles most car interiors and survives vibration and knocks.
- For a dashboard that gets full sun all day, ASA or ABS gives more margin.
