---
title: Phone case or bumper
summary: Needs to absorb drops and stay snug.
useCases: [flexible, functional]
best: tpu-95a
runnersUp: [tpu-85a]
avoid: [pla, silk-pla, petg]
---

- Only TPU gives the grip and shock absorption a case needs. 95A is firm enough to hold its shape and still flex over the phone; 85A gives a softer, more cushioning feel but is harder to print.
- Rigid filaments like PLA and PETG crack when you stretch them onto a phone.
