---
title: Decorative vase
summary: A showpiece where the finish is everything.
useCases: [display]
best: silk-pla
runnersUp: [matte-pla, pla]
avoid: []
---

- Vase mode (a single continuous wall) shows off silk PLA's metallic glow, and since a vase carries no load, silk's weaker layers do not matter.
- Matte PLA is the elegant alternative.
- Use a liner or a glass insert if it will hold real water, since vase-mode walls are not reliably watertight.
