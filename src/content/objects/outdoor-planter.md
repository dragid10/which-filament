---
title: Outdoor planter
summary: Sun, rain, heat, and temperature swings all day.
useCases: [outdoor, waterproof]
best: petg
runnersUp: [rapid-petg]
avoid: [pla, pla-plus, matte-pla, silk-pla, rapid-pla, rapid-pla-plus]
---

PETG tolerates UV, moisture, and heat far better than any PLA, so a planter that sits in a sunny spot will keep its shape for seasons. Print with thicker walls and good layer bonding so it stays watertight.

PLA in direct summer sun can soften and sag, and it slowly degrades outdoors. If it must be PLA, keep it in a shaded spot and expect a shorter life.
