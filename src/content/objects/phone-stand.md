---
title: Phone or tablet stand
summary: A desk stand that gets bumped, plugged into, and carried around.
useCases: [functional, beginner]
best: pla-plus
runnersUp: [pla, petg]
avoid: [silk-pla]
---

- A stand takes small knocks and constant handling, so a toughened PLA is the sweet spot: stiff enough to hold the device, less likely to snap at the thin back support than plain PLA.
- Plain PLA works fine if it lives on a desk.
- Choose PETG if it will get warm, such as on a car dashboard.
- Skip silk: its weak layers crack at the hinge point.
