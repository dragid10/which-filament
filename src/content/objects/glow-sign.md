---
title: Night light or glow sign
summary: A decoration that glows after the lights go off.
useCases: [display]
best: glow-pla
runnersUp: []
avoid: [pla-cf]
---

- Glow-in-the-dark PLA is the obvious pick.
- Use thick walls and solid infill so there is more pigment to glow, and charge it with a bright light before use.
- It is weak and brittle, so keep it as a decoration and not a load-bearing part.
- Remember to use a hardened nozzle.
