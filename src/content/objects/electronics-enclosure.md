---
title: Electronics enclosure
summary: Holds a warm board, takes knocks, and may need drilling or smoothing.
useCases: [functional, hot-environment]
best: abs
runnersUp: [petg, asa]
avoid: [pla, silk-pla]
---

- ABS tolerates the warmth of electronics, drills and sands cleanly for cutouts, and can be vapor-smoothed.
- Use it if you have an enclosed printer.
- PETG is the easier alternative on an open printer.
- Use ASA if the enclosure will sit outside.
