---
title: Jigs, fixtures, and stiff mounts
summary: Parts that must hold dimension and not flex under light load.
useCases: [functional, mechanical]
best: pla-cf
runnersUp: [pla-plus, petg-cf]
avoid: [glow-pla, wood-pla, silk-pla]
---

- Stiffness matters more than toughness for jigs, camera mounts, and fixtures.
- PLA-CF prints with very little warping and holds dimensions well.
- If you do not have a hardened nozzle, PLA+ is a good cheaper choice.
- Use PETG-CF if it needs a bit more heat tolerance, or ASA-CF for long outdoor use.
