---
title: Drone or RC vehicle frame
summary: Crashes, vibration, and stiffness requirements.
useCases: [mechanical, functional]
best: petg-cf
runnersUp: [petg, pla-cf]
avoid: [silk-pla, glow-pla, pla]
---

You want a stiff frame that survives crashes. PETG-CF gives stiffness with better heat and weather resistance than PLA-CF, and PETG gives a tougher but more flexible alternative if you want it cheaper.

Avoid anything brittle: silk and glow PLA shatter on impact.
