---
title: ASA
fullName: Acrylonitrile styrene acrylate
summary: Like ABS but built for the outdoors. Resists UV, weather, and heat without yellowing or cracking.
order: 4
pros:
  - "Excellent UV and weather resistance, so it stays put in sun, rain, and cold"
  - "Heat deflection around 86-96 °C, similar to or slightly below ABS"
  - "Tough with a clean, slightly matte finish"
  - "Smoothable with acetone like ABS"
cons:
  - "Needs an enclosure; prone to warping in drafts (sources rate it a little easier than ABS)"
  - "Gives off fumes while printing, so vent the enclosure outdoors"
  - "Costs more than ABS and PETG"
  - "Less beginner friendly than PLA or PETG"
settings:
  nozzle: 240-270 °C
  bed: 90-110 °C
  enclosure: Required
  drying: 70-80 °C for 4-6 h
sources:

---

ASA is what you reach for when ABS would be perfect except that the part lives outside. Car exterior parts, garden hardware, sensor housings, and outdoor brackets all suit it.

Print it the way you would ABS: enclosed, warm chamber, ventilated room.
