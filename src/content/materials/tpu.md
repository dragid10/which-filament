---
title: TPU
fullName: Thermoplastic polyurethane
summary: Flexible, rubbery filament for parts that bend, squash, and absorb impacts.
order: 5
homeUse: easy
homeNote: "Works best on a direct-drive extruder. Bowden printers can manage 95A at slow speeds."
pros:
  - "Truly flexible, and can stretch, bend, and bounce back"
  - "Very durable: resists impacts, abrasion, and repeated flexing"
  - "Good vibration damping and grip"
  - "Needs no enclosure"
cons:
  - "Prints slowly (often 20-40 mm/s), and fast printing causes jams"
  - "Stringing is common, and bridging and overhangs are weak"
  - "Easiest on a direct-drive extruder; Bowden setups can jam"
  - "Absorbs moisture, so keep it dry"
settings:
  nozzle: 210-240 °C
  bed: 30-60 °C
  enclosure: Not needed
  drying: 50-60 °C for 6-8 h

---

- The answer whenever the part has to bend: cases, gaskets, bumpers, grips, tires.
- The number on the spool is Shore hardness. Lower is softer and harder to print.
- A spool marked only "TPU" is usually 95A. Start there.
